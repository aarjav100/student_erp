# 🎓 Student Dropout Analysis & ERP System

> **Live Demo:** [student-erp-dusky.vercel.app](https://student-erp-dusky.vercel.app)

---

## 📌 Overview

This module is designed to monitor student academic progress, attendance records, fee statuses, and overall engagement to identify potential dropout risks early.

---

## 🎯 Key Capabilities

- **Attendance Monitoring:** Tracks daily attendance and triggers automatic alerts for low attendance thresholds.
- **Academic Performance Analysis:** Logs quiz attempts, assignments, and test grades to flag dropping scores.
- **Hostel & Fee Management:** Keeps track of pending dues and hostel leave patterns.
- **Early Intervention:** Provides administrators and teachers with actionable insights to assist students in need.
